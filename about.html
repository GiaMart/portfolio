Hi, I'm Gia Martini — a Digital Transformation & Systems Coordinator who designs clean visuals and builds practical systems that solve real business problems.

At Sterling Securities, I identify operational problems, design the solution, and use AI tools — especially Cursor and ChatGPT — to build and iterate on employee portals, inventory systems, and internal workflows.

I'm not a traditional developer. I'm a problem-solver who knows how to work with AI: writing clear prompts, reviewing output, testing in production, and refining until it works. That approach took me from Google Sites and AppSheet to a full custom employee portal deployed on a live domain.
