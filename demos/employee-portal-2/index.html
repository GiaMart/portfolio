    👔 Uniform Request

    Request shirts, pants, and hats. Please read the instructions before submitting.

    Before You Submit

    • Uniforms are issued in limited quantities. If our records show you have already received 2–3 sets, additional requests may not be fulfilled.

    • If you are unsure of your size, do not request multiple sizes. You must visit the Verona office to try items on.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Payroll, pay stubs, uniforms, and office info.

    💵 When Do We Get Paid?

    You get paid every other week (biweekly). Each paycheck covers a 2-week period of work, paid the following Friday.

    We send payroll at the start of the week, and most people get direct deposit on Friday. Some banks send the money early depending on your bank.

    If you don't use direct deposit, you can pick up your paper check at the Verona office on Friday. 📅 See the pay calendar on the for period start, end, and payday dates.

    📄 Where Can I Find My Pay Stubs?

    Pay stubs are available through the ADP mobile app or workforcenow.adp.com. You should have received an email invitation when you were hired.

    Once your account is set up, you can log in anytime to view, download, or print current and past pay statements.

    💳 Payroll Issues & Direct Deposit Changes

    Direct deposit changes must be made in person at the Verona office (Mon–Fri, 9 AM – 4 PM). This cannot be done through the portal.

    For payroll questions, contact the office during business hours. During payroll weeks, details may not be available until Friday after pay is processed.

    📞 What Is the Supervisor Hotline?

    The Supervisor Hotline connects you with a Sterling supervisor when you need help on short notice. Demo: (555) 010-0200

    🛟 Need Help With the Portal?

    Email the tech desk with your name and a short description of the issue. Screenshots are helpful if you can attach them.

    🏢 Where Can I Pick Up My Uniform or Paycheck?

    Uniforms and paper checks can be picked up at the main office during weekday hours. After submitting a uniform request, a supervisor may drop items at your site, but that is not guaranteed.

    Paper paychecks are ready on Fridays.
