About

Who We Are

Sterling Securities is a licensed, bonded, and fully insured private security company based in Verona, New Jersey, serving clients nationwide.

Led by retired law enforcement professionals with over 50 years of combined experience, Sterling Securities brings extensive knowledge, professionalism, and a commitment to safety to every assignment.

Our team includes experienced security officers, active and retired police officers, and law enforcement supervisors. We take pride in providing dependable, high-quality security services tailored to the unique needs of each client.

From protecting people and property to developing customized security solutions, our priority is delivering reliable protection you can trust.
