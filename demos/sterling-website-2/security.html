Security Guard Division

Sterling Securities provides professional security services tailored to the needs of each client. From uniformed to plainclothes officers, armed or unarmed, our team is committed to delivering dependable protection and exceptional service.

Sterling Securities offers armed and unarmed security personnel for residential, commercial, educational, and other properties throughout New Jersey.

Our services include on-site security officers, mobile patrols, access monitoring, event coverage, and specialized assignments based on the requirements of each location.

Armed & Unarmed Guards

Professional, highly trained security personnel available for short-term and long-term assignments, ensuring the safety of businesses, properties, and personnel.

Event Security

Expert crowd management and security services for corporate events, concerts, private gatherings, and public functions.

Residential Security

Protection for residential communities, apartment complexes, and private estates with patrol services and access control.

School & Campus Security

Security solutions for schools, universities, and campuses to enhance student and staff safety through patrols, emergency planning, and threat mitigation.
